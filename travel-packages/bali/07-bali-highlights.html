KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

  • Bali follows a tropical rhythm, so brief rain showers can arrive even during the drier months; keep plans flexible and enjoy the slower moments.
  • Temple visits require respectful clothing that covers shoulders and knees, while a sarong and sash are commonly provided at major temples.
  • The Indonesian rupiah is used locally. Carry small notes for village cafés, market purchases, parking and small entrance contributions.
  • Road journeys can take longer than map estimates because of village ceremonies, narrow lanes and busy southern Bali traffic.
  • Use reef-safe habits around the coast: carry a refillable bottle, avoid single-use plastic and never touch coral or marine life.
  • Travel insurance, a copy of your passport and a small personal medical kit are recommended for every traveller.

THINGS TO CARRY

  • Light cotton clothing and one respectful temple outfit with covered shoulders and knees.
  • Comfortable walking shoes, sandals, hat, sunglasses and a light rain layer.

  • Swimwear, quick-dry clothing and a dry pouch for coastal or waterfall visits.
