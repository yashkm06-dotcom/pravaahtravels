DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1

Title: Arrive in Hanoi | A Gentle Capital Welcome

Location: Noi Bai International Airport - Hanoi Old Quarter

Description: Meet the local team, settle into Hanoi and take an easy first walk beside the lake or through the Old Quarter.

Arrive at Noi Bai International Airport, complete immigration and meet the Pravaah Travels representative at the confirmed exit point. The drive into Hanoi reveals the capital gradually, moving from the Red River outskirts into tree-lined avenues, compact neighbourhoods and the energetic streets around the historic centre. After hotel check-in, allow time to rest, refresh and organise local currency before choosing a gentle orientation suited to your arrival hour. You might walk beside Hoan Kiem Lake, pause for Vietnamese coffee or watch vendors prepare the Old Quarter for evening. Your coordinator explains safe street crossing, the next morning’s pickup, expected weather and practical details for the Halong Bay overnight bag. Dinner remains flexible, though a nearby restaurant can be reserved for guests who prefer an easy first meal. The day is deliberately unhurried: Hanoi becomes more rewarding when its sounds, aromas and street rhythms are absorbed without forcing a checklist immediately after a flight.

DAY 2

Title: Hanoi Heritage | Temples, Lakes & Old Quarter Flavours

Location: Temple of Literature - Hoan Kiem Lake - Hanoi Old Quarter

Description: Connect Hanoi’s dynastic learning, colonial layers and living food culture on a full guided day.

After breakfast, begin at the Temple of Literature, founded in the eleventh century and arranged around tranquil courtyards, gateways and stone stelae honouring successful scholars. Your guide introduces Vietnam’s Confucian educational tradition before continuing through the civic heart of Hanoi, where French-era buildings, shaded boulevards and memorial spaces reveal later chapters of the capital. Pause for a local lunch or a carefully selected street-food tasting, trying manageable portions such as fresh rolls, bun cha or an egg coffee according to preference. The afternoon turns toward Hoan Kiem Lake and the Old Quarter’s guild streets. Rather than rushing through the crowds, follow a focused walk past family shops, hidden communal houses and narrow facades adapted to centuries of commerce. There is time to ask about ingredients, customs and contemporary city life. End with a water-puppet performance or an independent lakeside evening, then prepare a small overnight bag for the next morning’s cruise transfer.

DAY 3

Title: Hanoi to Halong Bay | Cruise into a UNESCO Seascape

Location: Hanoi - Halong Bay cruise harbour - Halong Bay

Description: Board an overnight vessel and sail among extraordinary limestone islands, caves and sheltered water.

Leave Hanoi after breakfast for the cruise harbour, travelling through the Red River Delta as villages, rice fields and newer industrial towns pass the window. At the terminal, complete boarding formalities and meet the cruise team before settling into your assigned cabin. Lunch is served as the vessel moves away from shore and Halong Bay’s limestone karsts begin to fill the horizon. The afternoon activity is selected by the captain according to weather, tides and harbour approval; it may feature a cave walk, kayaking, a bamboo-boat excursion or time near a sheltered beach. Return aboard for refreshments and sunset on deck, when the formations soften into silhouettes and the bay becomes especially atmospheric. A Vietnamese and international dinner follows, with optional squid fishing, conversation in the lounge or quiet time on your balcony. Keep the evening relaxed and listen to the water around the boat. The experience combines UNESCO geology with the simple pleasure of travelling slowly through a remarkable landscape.

DAY 4

Title: Halong Dawn & Flight to Da Nang

Location: Halong Bay - Hanoi airport - Da Nang

Description: Wake on the bay, complete the cruise programme and fly south to Vietnam’s relaxed central coast.

Rise early if you would like to join tai chi or simply watch first light reveal layers of karst through the morning haze. A light breakfast precedes the final cruise activity, which may visit another cavern, viewpoint or quiet inlet depending on the confirmed vessel route. Back aboard, enjoy brunch while the ship returns toward the harbour and the crew completes check-out. Your driver meets you for the road transfer to Hanoi airport, with a practical timing buffer built around the domestic flight. On arrival in Da Nang, collect your luggage and meet the central Vietnam representative for the transfer to the hotel. The contrast is immediate: broad roads, the Han River, sea air and a more open skyline replace Hanoi’s compressed energy. Check in and keep the evening free for a beach walk or a fresh seafood dinner. The schedule avoids major touring after the combined cruise and flight, preserving energy for the heritage and coastal experiences ahead.

DAY 5

Title: Da Nang Coast | My Khe Beach & Riverside Life

Location: My Khe Beach - Son Tra viewpoints - Han River, Da Nang

Description: Enjoy a restorative central-coast day with beach time, river views and an introduction to modern Da Nang.

Begin at an easy pace with breakfast and time beside My Khe Beach, whose long sandy shoreline gives Da Nang its bright, open character. Conditions permitting, swim in the designated area or simply walk at the water’s edge while local residents exercise and fishing boats sit offshore. Later, join a city orientation taking in the Han River, the sculptural bridges and selected viewpoints toward the Son Tra Peninsula. Your guide explains how Da Nang developed from a historic port into one of Vietnam’s most liveable contemporary cities. Lunch can focus on central dishes such as mi quang or fresh seafood, with spice adjusted to taste. The afternoon remains flexible for the hotel pool, an optional Marble Mountains visit or café time. As evening arrives, return to the riverfront to see the bridges illuminated and choose a relaxed dinner. This lighter day gives the journey an important pause between Halong’s cruise schedule and Hoi An’s heritage-rich lanes.

DAY 6

Title: Hoi An Ancient Town | Merchant Stories & Lantern Light

Location: Da Nang - Hoi An Ancient Town - Thu Bon River

Description: Explore UNESCO-listed Hoi An with a heritage walk, local craft encounters and a lantern-lit river evening.

Travel south to Hoi An after breakfast and enter the ancient town before the busiest afternoon period. With your guide, examine timber merchant houses, tiled roofs, assembly halls and the Japanese Covered Bridge area while learning how Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese and European trading influences met at this river port. Pause in a family-run workshop or small museum to understand a local craft, then enjoy lunch featuring central Vietnamese flavours. Free time follows for tailoring consultations, galleries, tea shops or a slow walk along the Thu Bon River; purchases are entirely optional and quality questions are welcomed. Near sunset, the ochre streets begin to glow under silk lanterns. Join a short lantern boat when river and safety conditions allow, or appreciate the scene from the promenade if you prefer to remain ashore. Dinner can be reserved in a restored heritage building before the return to Da Nang or an overnight Hoi An hotel, according to the selected plan.

DAY 7

Title: Central Vietnam at Leisure | Beach, Craft or Countryside

Location: Da Nang and Hoi An region

Description: Personalise a free day with beach relaxation, a cooking experience, village cycling or additional heritage time.

Today is intentionally open so the first-time route includes personal discovery rather than constant group movement. Beach-focused guests can enjoy the resort, My Khe shoreline and a long lunch overlooking the sea. Travellers interested in food may add a market visit and hands-on cooking class, learning how herbs, rice paper and balanced sauces shape central Vietnamese cuisine. A gentle countryside option can combine village lanes, vegetable gardens and a short basket-boat experience, subject to water conditions and responsible local operation. Those drawn back to Hoi An can return for tailoring fittings, museums or quieter photography beyond the main lanes. Your coordinator helps compare travel time, weather and energy before confirming any optional activity; nothing is imposed. Reassemble in the evening for an independent dinner or a farewell meal included in selected plans. The flexible day lets couples, friends and families finish the journey in the style that matters most to them.

DAY 8

Title: Da Nang Farewell | Departure

Location: Da Nang hotel - Da Nang International Airport

Description: Enjoy a final breakfast and transfer to the airport with generous time for the onward flight.

Wake for a final breakfast and, if flight time permits, take a last walk beside the sea or enjoy an unhurried Vietnamese coffee. Complete hotel check-out after checking wardrobes, the room safe and charging points, keeping passports and domestic-flight receipts accessible. Your representative reconfirms the airport terminal and arranges the selected shared or private vehicle with a sensible allowance for city traffic and airline procedures. The short journey to Da Nang International Airport closes a route of memorable contrasts: Hanoi’s scholarly courtyards and lively food lanes, Halong Bay’s sculpted limestone horizon, Da Nang’s coastal openness and Hoi An’s lantern-lit merchant heritage. Guests continuing elsewhere in Vietnam can connect toward Ho Chi Minh City, Hue or a longer beach stay, while international departures receive the appropriate timing guidance. The package ends at the airport, but the destination team remains reachable for service questions. Leave with a confident first understanding of Vietnam and several clear reasons to explore its regions more deeply.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

  • Northern and central Vietnam can experience different weather during the same journey; pack for sun, rain and cooler cruise evenings.
  • Halong Bay routes and activities depend on harbour authority, visibility, tides and safe operating conditions.
  • The Hanoi-Da Nang domestic flight schedule can change, and the final day order will protect adequate airport time.
  • Hoi An Ancient Town is best explored on foot; uneven paving and warm weather require comfortable footwear and hydration.
  • Street traffic can feel intense in Hanoi, so cross steadily with local guidance and avoid sudden movements.
  • Cards work at major hotels, but small vendors and markets generally prefer Vietnamese dong.
  • Ask before photographing people, ceremonies, homes or working artisans.
  • Travel insurance should cover medical needs, weather disruption and domestic transport changes.
