Full Description: Northern Vietnam has a distinctive mood: scholarly courtyards, mist-softened limestone, old commercial lanes and food traditions built around balance and freshness. This focused itinerary stays within the region so Hanoi and Halong Bay can be experienced with depth. It is ideal for travellers who prefer an immersive short holiday to a fast sequence of domestic flights.

Hanoi is more than a gateway to the bay. Food offers a direct route into northern culture. With your guide, sample selected dishes such as bun cha, pho, crisp pancakes or egg coffee, adapting the tasting for allergies and comfort. The objective is not novelty for its own sake, but an understanding of herbs, broths, rice, smoke and texture. DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1

Title: Arrive in Hanoi | Old Quarter First Impressions

Location: Noi Bai International Airport - central Hanoi

Description: Transfer into the capital and ease into its lake, cafés and animated historic streets.

After immigration at Noi Bai International Airport, meet the representative holding the confirmed arrival sign and travel into central Hanoi. The road crosses the Red River and gradually enters a denser world of neighbourhood markets, shade trees, scooters and slender buildings. Check in at the selected hotel and leave the first hours open for rest, particularly after an overnight international flight. Later, the coordinator can lead a brief orientation toward Hoan Kiem Lake or recommend an easy independent route through the edge of the Old Quarter. Pause for a robust Vietnamese coffee, watch residents gather beside the water and notice how temples, boutiques and street kitchens share the same urban space. A welcome dinner can be arranged under the Deluxe or Luxury plan, while Standard guests receive trusted nearby suggestions. Before retiring, review the next day’s pickup and keep valuables secure. The arrival is intentionally gentle, giving you time to become comfortable with Hanoi’s energy before the deeper guided exploration begins.

DAY 2

Title: Hanoi in Depth | Scholarship, Streets & Northern Cuisine

Location: Temple of Literature - French Quarter - Old Quarter

Description: Read Hanoi’s history through its scholarly courtyards, architectural layers and carefully chosen local flavours.

Begin at the Temple of Literature, where formal gateways and garden courtyards once prepared scholars for an education grounded in Confucian thought. Your guide decodes the layout, stone doctoral stelae and importance of learning in Vietnamese public life. Continue past selected monuments and French-era buildings, discussing the complex political and architectural changes that shaped modern Hanoi. Lunch or a late tasting focuses on northern specialities: perhaps smoky bun cha, fresh rice rolls, a fragrant bowl of pho or egg coffee, chosen with dietary needs in mind. The afternoon belongs to the Old Quarter. Walk a purposeful route through guild streets, family-run shops and hidden communal spaces rather than trying to cover every lane. Learn how the narrow houses evolved and how commerce continues behind their colourful facades. Conclude beside Hoan Kiem Lake, with an optional water-puppet performance or free time for dinner. The day combines major heritage with the everyday details that make Hanoi feel lived in rather than staged.

DAY 3

Title: Optional Ninh Binh | Rivers, Rice Fields & Limestone Peaks

Location: Hanoi - Ninh Binh - Hanoi

Description: Choose a scenic Ninh Binh excursion or remain in Hanoi for independent museums, cafés and neighbourhood exploration.

Guests selecting Ninh Binh leave early for a drive south through the Red River Delta, observing village life, cultivated fields and the mountains appearing beyond the plain. The confirmed route may combine a historic temple complex with a sampan-style rowing journey at Trang An or Tam Coc. Local boaters guide the craft along calm waterways beneath limestone walls and, when water levels permit, through low natural caves. A Vietnamese lunch provides a comfortable break before a gentle countryside viewpoint or village stop. Respect the rower, follow life-jacket guidance and avoid leaving waste in this sensitive landscape. Travellers who do not add the excursion enjoy an independent Hanoi day, perhaps visiting the Women’s Museum, exploring contemporary galleries, booking a spa treatment or lingering over lunch in the French Quarter. Both choices protect an unhurried evening. Pack a smaller bag for Halong Bay and leave unnecessary large luggage securely with the hotel before the next morning’s harbour departure.

DAY 4

Title: Luxury Halong Bay Cruise | Limestone, Water & Sunset

Location: Hanoi - Halong Bay cruise terminal - Halong Bay

Description: Board the selected vessel for an overnight UNESCO voyage featuring beautiful scenery, full-board dining and a guided activity.

Depart Hanoi on the scheduled private or shared transfer, crossing the delta toward the coast and Halong cruise terminal. After lounge formalities, board by tender or gangway as instructed and meet the crew for a safety briefing. Your cabin category reflects the chosen package, from a comfortable window cabin to a spacious balcony suite, but every plan follows the same essential UNESCO landscape. Lunch arrives as the vessel sails among increasingly dramatic limestone towers. In the afternoon, join the captain-approved programme, which may include a cave visit, kayaking, swimming or a bamboo boat through a sheltered area. The route can change with tides, weather and port regulation. Return for sunset on deck, a cooking demonstration or quiet private time before dinner. As other boats settle for the night, the bay takes on a calm that day visitors rarely experience. Optional squid fishing or lounge conversation follows, though many guests simply enjoy the dark silhouettes beyond the cabin glass.

DAY 5

Title: Halong Sunrise | Return to Hanoi

Location: Halong Bay - cruise terminal - Hanoi

Description: Welcome the bay at first light, complete the cruise route and return to Hanoi for a relaxed final evening.

Wake early for tai chi on deck or take a private moment with tea as the first light reaches the limestone formations. The cool morning air and quiet water often provide the most reflective part of the cruise. After a light breakfast, join the final scheduled activity, perhaps a cavern, island viewpoint or small-boat route selected for the day’s conditions. Return to the vessel, freshen up and complete cabin check-out before an early brunch is served on the journey back to port. Say farewell to the crew and meet the driver for Hanoi, allowing for a comfort stop along the way. Once at the city hotel, collect stored luggage and settle in for the final night. The remainder of the day is free for souvenir shopping, a massage, a lakeside walk or a farewell dinner. Luxury guests may have a chef-led reservation, while other plans receive curated suggestions. The evening balances the bay’s tranquillity with one last taste of capital life.

DAY 6

Title: Hanoi Farewell | Departure

Location: Hanoi hotel - Noi Bai International Airport

Description: Complete the northern Vietnam escape with a smooth airport transfer and time for a final coffee when the flight permits.

Enjoy breakfast at the hotel and use the available morning according to flight time. A final circuit of Hoan Kiem Lake, a quiet coffee or a visit to a trusted craft shop can be arranged without risking the airport schedule. Complete check-out, verify that passports and e-visa records are easy to reach and check that cruise purchases comply with airline baggage rules. Your driver departs with a realistic allowance for Hanoi traffic and international check-in procedures. Looking back, the six days have presented northern Vietnam through two complementary experiences: the human density and historical layers of Hanoi, and the vast natural composition of Halong Bay. Guests who selected Ninh Binh add a third perspective in the delta’s rivers and fields. The package concludes at Noi Bai International Airport, where you continue home or connect to central or southern Vietnam. The local team remains available for urgent travel support until departure, closing a compact journey that has never felt superficial.
