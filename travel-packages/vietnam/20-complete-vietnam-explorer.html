DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1

Title: Arrive in Hanoi | The North-to-South Journey Begins

Location: Noi Bai International Airport - central Hanoi

Description: Settle into the capital and receive a relaxed introduction to its lakes, lanes and practical rhythm.

Arrive at Noi Bai International Airport, present the required travel documents and meet the representative after baggage collection. The transfer toward central Hanoi crosses the Red River and moves gradually from outer districts into a dense streetscape of scooters, shade trees, cafés and narrow townhouses. Check in at the selected hotel and rest before an optional orientation around Hoan Kiem Lake. The coordinator explains safe street crossing, local cash, the multi-region baggage plan and the next morning’s pickup. If energy permits, sip Vietnamese coffee, explore the edge of the Old Quarter or choose a gentle dinner from a curated list. Guests on premium plans may have an included welcome experience, while Standard travellers retain complete flexibility. Avoid scheduling a major monument on arrival day; the ten-day itinerary has enough depth without exhausting the first evening. Pack valuables securely and let the capital’s sounds and aromas become the opening notes of the complete Vietnam journey.

DAY 2

Title: Hanoi Heritage | Scholars, Guilds & Street Flavours

Location: Temple of Literature - central Hanoi - Old Quarter

Description: Explore the capital’s intellectual history, colonial layers and living Old Quarter food culture.

Begin after breakfast at the Temple of Literature, a sequence of gates and courtyards associated with Vietnam’s long scholarly tradition. Your guide explains the doctoral stelae, Confucian influences and the site’s continuing place in student life. Continue through selected civic districts, comparing older Vietnamese forms with French-era planning and the political landmarks of modern Hanoi. A curated lunch or walking tasting introduces northern flavours such as bun cha, rice rolls, fresh herbs or egg coffee, adapted thoughtfully to dietary needs. In the Old Quarter, follow a focused route through guild streets, family shops and small communal sites. The experience prioritises context over speed, revealing how commerce, worship and domestic life share limited urban space. End beside Hoan Kiem Lake with time for photographs, an optional water-puppet performance or independent dinner. Back at the hotel, prepare the overnight cruise bag while larger luggage is labelled for secure storage or onward handling.

DAY 3

Title: Hanoi to Halong Bay | Overnight UNESCO Cruise

Location: Hanoi - Halong cruise terminal - Halong Bay

Description: Leave the capital for a full-board voyage among the bay’s extraordinary limestone islands.

Travel east after breakfast through the Red River Delta to the Halong Bay cruise terminal. Following check-in and a safety briefing, board the confirmed vessel and settle into a window cabin, private-balcony cabin or premium suite according to plan. Lunch is served as urban shorelines fade and limestone towers begin to surround the route. The afternoon programme is determined by the captain, harbour authority, tide and weather; it may include a cave, kayaking, swimming or a bamboo-boat excursion through a protected area. Return to the vessel for sunset refreshments and time on deck, appreciating how light changes the apparent shape and distance of the karsts. Dinner presents Vietnamese and international choices, with dietary requests prearranged. Optional squid fishing or a lounge activity follows, although the most memorable pleasure is often the bay’s quiet after darkness. Overnight aboard with the vessel anchored in its permitted area, ready for an early morning on the water.

DAY 4

Title: Halong Dawn & Flight to Da Nang

Location: Halong Bay - Hanoi airport - Da Nang

Description: Complete the cruise at sunrise, return to Hanoi and fly to the open landscapes of the central coast.

Join tai chi on deck or watch sunrise privately as the bay emerges through changing mist. A light breakfast is followed by the vessel’s final activity, selected from the approved route and current conditions. Back aboard, complete cabin check-out and enjoy brunch while cruising toward the terminal. Your driver meets the disembarkation and continues toward Hanoi airport with a generous buffer for traffic and domestic procedures. Fly to Da Nang, where the central Vietnam team assists with luggage and the transfer to the coast. Broad roads, the Han River and the sea create an immediate contrast with Hanoi’s compressed streets and Halong’s isolated islands. Check in and keep the evening deliberately light after a cruise, road journey and flight. Walk by My Khe Beach, order a bowl of central noodles or rest at the hotel. The operations team reconfirms the next day’s plan, adjusting outdoor timing for heat or rain while protecting the essential Da Nang and Hoi An experiences.

DAY 5

Title: Da Nang | My Khe Beach & Coastal City Life

Location: My Khe Beach - Han River - Da Nang

Description: Enjoy the central coast through beach time, a city orientation and a relaxed introduction to regional cuisine.

Start early at My Khe Beach, when local residents exercise beside the water and the tropical sun remains gentle. Swim only within designated areas and follow safety flags, or choose a long shoreline walk followed by breakfast. Later, join an orientation connecting the beach with selected Son Tra views and Da Nang’s Han River bridges. The guide explains the city’s maritime roots, rapid development and role as a gateway between former imperial Hue and the trading port of Hoi An. Lunch can feature mi quang noodles or responsibly sourced seafood, with vegetarian options available. The afternoon is free for the resort pool, a spa treatment, café exploration or an optional Marble Mountains visit. Avoid overloading the schedule, as the complete explorer route benefits from a restorative coastal pause. Near evening, see the riverfront illuminate and select an independent meal. Premium plans may include a signature dinner or wellness ritual, while all guests receive clear arrangements for the following Hoi An heritage day.

DAY 6

Title: Hoi An Ancient Town | Trading Heritage & Lantern Evening

Location: Da Nang - Hoi An - Thu Bon River

Description: Discover Hoi An’s UNESCO merchant legacy, craft traditions and luminous riverside atmosphere.

Travel to Hoi An and begin a guided walk among the ancient town’s ochre walls, timber merchant houses and community assembly halls. Your guide traces the port’s commercial connections and the ways Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese and European influences appear in architecture, ritual and food. Enter selected heritage buildings, noticing internal courtyards, carved beams, family altars and historic flood marks. After a central Vietnamese lunch, enjoy free time for a craft workshop, tailoring consultation, museum or shaded café, with no compulsory shopping. As evening approaches, silk lanterns appear above the lanes and along the Thu Bon River. Join a brief river outing when it is safely operating, or appreciate the reflections from the promenade. A selected dinner is included in higher plans before returning to Da Nang or staying locally under the confirmed hotel pattern. The day shows why Hoi An’s appeal extends beyond its photogenic colour: it remains a living record of exchange, resilience and community.

DAY 7

Title: Fly to Ho Chi Minh City | Southern Energy

Location: Da Nang airport - Ho Chi Minh City

Description: Travel south and orient yourself among the boulevards, markets and contemporary pulse of Vietnam’s largest city.

After breakfast, check out and transfer to Da Nang International Airport for the scheduled flight to Ho Chi Minh City. Standard baggage allowance is included within the selected airfare provision, while additional weight or seat preferences are charged at live cost. On arrival, meet the southern representative and travel toward the central hotel through a cityscape that feels denser, warmer and more commercially driven than the previous regions. After check-in, take a guided or independent orientation along a central boulevard, around the exterior of key colonial-era landmarks or through a nearby market district according to arrival time. Your guide introduces the continuing use of the name Saigon, explains the city’s district structure and recommends safe evening dining. Try southern flavours, visit a contemporary rooftop or rest after the flight. The day is designed as a transition, allowing Ho Chi Minh City’s energy to register before the more detailed historical and neighbourhood tour begins the next morning.

DAY 8

Title: Ho Chi Minh City | History, Markets & Modern Ambition

Location: Central Ho Chi Minh City and selected neighbourhoods

Description: Read the southern metropolis through historic landmarks and food, with a personalised afternoon or pre-booked Cu Chi alternative.

Set out after breakfast for a considered city tour combining exterior and interior visits according to current access. See landmarks such as the Central Post Office, selected colonial-era facades and civic boulevards while discussing the city’s complex twentieth-century history. A museum visit may add deeper context, with the guide preparing guests for sensitive material and allowing personal pace. Continue toward a market or neighbourhood food area to observe the ingredients, small businesses and trading habits behind southern cuisine. Lunch offers dishes with the region’s characteristic sweetness, herbs and tropical produce. The afternoon balances another cultural stop with free time for cafés, design shops, speciality coffee, wellness or the hotel pool. DAY 9

Title: Mekong Delta | Waterways, Orchards & Local Enterprise

Location: Ho Chi Minh City - Mekong Delta - Ho Chi Minh City

Description: Travel into the fertile delta for river journeys, family industries and a hosted regional lunch.

Depart early for the Mekong Delta, leaving Ho Chi Minh City’s dense suburbs for flatter agricultural landscapes crossed by canals and rivers. Board a licensed boat on the main waterway, then transfer to a smaller craft where current level, safe access and local operation permit. The guide explains how sediment, seasonal water and an intricate channel network support fruit orchards, rice production, transport and family businesses. Visit a responsibly selected workshop, garden or village enterprise without expecting staged performances, and taste seasonal produce when available. A hosted lunch introduces delta flavours in a shaded local setting. Weather and tide may change the exact route, and guests should use handrails and life jackets as directed. Return by road in the late afternoon, watching rural scenery gradually give way to the southern metropolis. Keep the evening quiet or choose a farewell drink, carrying a more grounded understanding of the water system that sustains millions of people.
