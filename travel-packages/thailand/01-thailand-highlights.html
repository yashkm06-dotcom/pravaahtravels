DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1

Title: Arrive in Bangkok and settle into the city

Location: Bangkok

Description: Arrive at Suvarnabhumi Airport, where your representative will welcome you and assist with the transfer to your hotel. After check-in, keep the first afternoon easy so your body can adjust to the climate and the pace of the city. Take a short walk around the neighbourhood, visit a nearby cafe or rest beside the pool. In the evening, your tour manager will share the plan for the coming days and useful local guidance about transport, dress and dining. If energy permits, explore a night market for fresh fruit, grilled seafood and mango sticky rice. Overnight in Bangkok with the city lights beginning your Thailand story.

The first day is deliberately light so guests can recover from their journey and settle into the local rhythm. Keep passports and medicines in hand luggage, drink water after the flight and use the hotel team for practical questions. A short neighbourhood walk is enough if arrival is late. The coordinator will share the next morning's departure time, breakfast arrangement and any clothing guidance for temples or nature activities.

DAY 2

Title: Bangkok temples, river life and evening flavours

Location: Bangkok

Description: Start with Bangkok's historic heart. Visit the Grand Palace complex and learn about the ceremonies, architecture and stories connected with the former royal residence. Continue to Wat Pho, known for its reclining Buddha and traditional massage school, then cross the river to Wat Arun, whose porcelain details glow beautifully in the morning light. A short boat ride along the Chao Phraya reveals old wooden homes, riverside shrines and the daily rhythm of local ferries. After lunch, take time to rest or browse a design market. As evening arrives, enjoy a guided street-food walk where you can sample noodles, grilled skewers, tropical fruit and coconut desserts. Return to the hotel after a full but comfortable day.

Start with breakfast and a clear meeting point before the main touring begins. The guide will keep the pace comfortable, explain local etiquette and point out places to pause for water or a snack. Guests should carry only what they need for the day and keep valuables secure. Evening plans remain flexible, allowing the group to choose a market, a simple dinner or an early night after the sightseeing.

DAY 3

Title: Floating market and transfer to Pattaya

Location: Bangkok, Pattaya

Description: Leave the city after breakfast for a morning market experience. Depending on the operating schedule, visit a lively floating market or a traditional canal-side market where boats, produce and small kitchens create a vivid scene. Your guide will help you understand what to taste and how to shop respectfully. Continue towards Pattaya after lunch, with a stop at a viewpoint or a quiet coastal temple on the way when conditions allow. Check in at the Pattaya hotel and enjoy the remainder of the afternoon at your own pace. Walk along the promenade at sunset, try a seafood dinner or simply relax near the water. The evening is intentionally open so you can choose the coastal experience that suits you.

This transition day connects two different sides of Thailand, so the timing is practical rather than rushed. Keep a small bag accessible during the transfer and label larger luggage clearly. On arrival, the coordinator will explain the hotel area, nearby dining choices and the following day's meeting point. A relaxed evening helps everyone enjoy the next excursion with more energy.

DAY 4

Title: Coral Island beach escape

Location: Pattaya, Coral Island

Description: Transfer to the pier after breakfast for a boat ride towards Coral Island. The day is designed around sea air and easy exploration rather than a fixed rush of activities. Swim in the clear shallows, relax on the sand, or choose an optional water activity from the local operators. Please follow safety instructions and use reef-friendly sunscreen. Enjoy lunch with a view before returning to Pattaya in the afternoon. Back at the hotel, there is time for a shower and rest. Later, take a gentle walk through the waterfront or visit a small cafe for Thai iced tea. The combination of bright water, warm sand and a slow evening makes this one of the most relaxed days of the trip.

The day is paced around the best available light and the comfort of the group. Walking surfaces may be uneven, and some viewpoints require steps, so suitable shoes are useful. Guests who prefer a quieter experience can spend more time at the main site while others continue with the guide. Local food, shade breaks and responsible photography are part of the experience.

DAY 5

Title: Pattaya to Phuket and beachside evening

Location: Pattaya, Phuket

Description: After breakfast, complete the hotel check-out and transfer to the airport for your flight to Phuket. On arrival, meet the local representative and travel to your hotel through green hills and coastal neighbourhoods. Once settled, use the afternoon for rest or a first look at the nearest beach. Phuket evenings are best enjoyed slowly: watch the changing light, order a fresh seafood dish and listen to the sea before exploring further. Your tour manager can suggest a suitable viewpoint or an easy local restaurant based on your interests. Keep valuables secure in busy areas and carry a light layer for air-conditioned transfers. Overnight in Phuket with the Andaman coast ready for the next day's island experience.

Keep the morning unhurried and ask the guide about the safest route for the current season. Markets and community visits are opportunities to buy directly from local makers and to learn before taking photographs. Carry small notes, water and a reusable bag. The afternoon is open so guests can rest, enjoy a massage or explore independently without losing the structure of the overall trip.

DAY 6

Title: Phuket island scenery and lagoons

Location: Phuket, Andaman Sea

Description: Set out after breakfast for a full-day island excursion. The exact route depends on sea conditions and the operating permit, but the experience centres on turquoise water, dramatic limestone formations, quiet bays and time to swim or float. Travel by a licensed boat and listen carefully to the crew's safety briefing. Between stops, enjoy the views of fishing boats and forested islands rising from the sea. Lunch is served during the excursion or at a selected island restaurant. Return to Phuket in the late afternoon and allow yourself a restful evening. Those who prefer land-based sightseeing may swap the boat experience for Old Phuket Town and a sunset viewpoint. Your guide will help select the best option for the weather.

The final touring day is a chance to revisit a favourite view or choose a small optional experience. Confirm departure arrangements early, especially if the next transfer connects with a flight. Pack purchases carefully, keep documents separate and allow time for traffic. The coordinator remains available for restaurant recommendations, airport questions and any last-minute practical support.

DAY 7

Title: Old Phuket Town and departure

Location: Phuket

Description: Enjoy a final breakfast and a relaxed morning before departure. If your flight timing permits, walk through Old Phuket Town to see colourful Sino-Portuguese buildings, small shrines, craft shops and local breakfast counters. Pick up a few practical gifts, take photographs respectfully and leave enough time for the airport transfer. Your representative will confirm the schedule and assist with luggage. The journey ends at Phuket International Airport with the memories of Bangkok's temples, Pattaya's coast and Phuket's island horizon. We recommend arriving early for international check-in and keeping travel documents, medication and valuables in your hand luggage. Depart Thailand with the freedom to return for a slower northern or southern island journey.

Allow the day to unfold around the water, weather and the interests of the group. A boat crew or local guide will explain boarding, swimming boundaries and the return time. Guests should protect phones, use sun protection and avoid touching coral or wildlife. The evening is reserved for an easy meal and a review of the next departure or transfer details.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

  • Thailand is warm and humid; carry breathable clothing, sunscreen and a refillable water bottle.
  • Shoulders and knees should be covered when visiting temples.
  • Boat excursions depend on sea and weather conditions and may change route for safety.
  • Keep small cash for markets, local snacks, tips and optional activities.
  • Use licensed taxis, registered tour boats and official hotel transfers.
  • Travel insurance with medical and water-activity cover is recommended.

THINGS TO CARRY

  • Passport, travel documents and a secure copy of important papers.
  • Light cotton clothes, modest temple clothing and a thin evening layer.
  • Comfortable walking shoes, sandals, hat and sunglasses.
  • Swimwear, quick-dry towel and reef-friendly sunscreen.
  • Personal medicines, basic first aid and insect repellent.
  • Universal adapter, power bank and waterproof phone pouch.
