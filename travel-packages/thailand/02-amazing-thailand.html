Spend a day on the water among island cliffs and quiet lagoons, then keep a full day free for beach time, Old Phuket Town or a cooking class. The holiday finishes with a smooth airport transfer and the sense that Thailand can be both vibrant and restorative. DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1

Title: Arrival in Bangkok

Location: Bangkok

Description: Welcome to Thailand. Meet your airport representative and transfer to your Bangkok hotel. The first day is kept gentle so you can recover from the journey and settle into the tropical climate. After check-in, enjoy a quiet drink, a swim or a walk around the hotel neighbourhood. Your coordinator will explain the next day's temple tour and help with practical questions about clothing, currency and local transport. In the evening, you may choose a riverside dinner or a nearby market. Rest well because Bangkok's historic quarter is waiting in the morning. Overnight in Bangkok.

The first day is deliberately light so guests can recover from their journey and settle into the local rhythm. Keep passports and medicines in hand luggage, drink water after the flight and use the hotel team for practical questions. A short neighbourhood walk is enough if arrival is late. The coordinator will share the next morning's departure time, breakfast arrangement and any clothing guidance for temples or nature activities.

DAY 2

Title: Bangkok heritage and the Chao Phraya

Location: Bangkok

Description: After breakfast, explore Bangkok's historic centre with a local guide. Visit the Grand Palace area, Wat Pho and Wat Arun, taking time to appreciate the details rather than rushing from one landmark to another. A short river crossing or ferry ride offers a different view of the city, with working boats, old homes and riverside shrines. Enjoy lunch at a local restaurant and return to the hotel in the afternoon. The rest of the day is free for a massage, modern shopping district or coffee in a converted shophouse. In the evening, sample a few dishes at a supervised street-food area. Wear respectful clothing at temples and keep water with you throughout the day.

Start with breakfast and a clear meeting point before the main touring begins. The guide will keep the pace comfortable, explain local etiquette and point out places to pause for water or a snack. Guests should carry only what they need for the day and keep valuables secure. Evening plans remain flexible, allowing the group to choose a market, a simple dinner or an early night after the sightseeing.

DAY 3

Title: Markets and Bangkok at leisure

Location: Bangkok

Description: Begin with a market outing outside the busiest central streets. Depending on the operating day, visit a floating market, railway market or a traditional fresh-produce market. Your guide will explain how local vendors work and help you choose safe, seasonal tastings. Return to Bangkok after lunch and keep the afternoon open. You can visit a museum, browse a design district or rest at the hotel. The tour team can arrange an optional dinner cruise or rooftop viewpoint, but there is no pressure to add another activity. This slower day is useful for guests who want to shop, photograph neighbourhood life or simply enjoy the city's energy at their own pace.

This transition day connects two different sides of Thailand, so the timing is practical rather than rushed. Keep a small bag accessible during the transfer and label larger luggage clearly. On arrival, the coordinator will explain the hotel area, nearby dining choices and the following day's meeting point. A relaxed evening helps everyone enjoy the next excursion with more energy.

DAY 4

Title: Fly to Chiang Mai and old city evening

Location: Bangkok, Chiang Mai

Description: Complete breakfast and check-out before your scheduled flight north. On arrival in Chiang Mai, meet the local representative and transfer to the hotel. The city feels calmer than Bangkok, with low-rise lanes, old walls, flowering courtyards and mountains visible beyond the rooftops. After check-in, take an easy walk through the old city and visit a small temple if open. In the evening, explore a local market for northern specialities such as khao soi, grilled meats, herbs and sticky rice. Keep some time for a traditional foot massage or a quiet cafe. Overnight in Chiang Mai and prepare for a hilltop temple visit tomorrow.

The day is paced around the best available light and the comfort of the group. Walking surfaces may be uneven, and some viewpoints require steps, so suitable shoes are useful. Guests who prefer a quieter experience can spend more time at the main site while others continue with the guide. Local food, shade breaks and responsible photography are part of the experience.

DAY 5

Title: Doi Suthep and Chiang Mai viewpoints

Location: Chiang Mai, Doi Suthep

Description: Travel up the forested road to Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, one of Chiang Mai's most important temples. Climb the steps at a comfortable pace and listen to the stories of the shrine, then pause for the broad view across the city and valley. Return towards town with a stop at a local community or craft workshop where makers work with textiles, paper, wood or ceramics. Lunch can be enjoyed in a family-run restaurant. Spend the afternoon resting or exploring the old city on foot. At sunset, choose a relaxed riverside cafe or a market stall dinner. The day combines spirituality, scenery and living craft traditions without an overfilled schedule.

Keep the morning unhurried and ask the guide about the safest route for the current season. Markets and community visits are opportunities to buy directly from local makers and to learn before taking photographs. Carry small notes, water and a reusable bag. The afternoon is open so guests can rest, enjoy a massage or explore independently without losing the structure of the overall trip.

DAY 6

Title: Northern nature and ethical experience

Location: Chiang Mai countryside

Description: Leave the city after breakfast for a countryside experience. The exact programme will be selected with a responsible local operator and may include a gentle forest walk, a village garden, a cooking lesson or an ethical elephant sanctuary that does not offer riding or forced performances. Learn about local food, water, farming and the importance of protecting the surrounding landscape. Lunch is served using regional ingredients before the return to Chiang Mai. The afternoon remains free so guests can rest after the outdoor experience. Please follow guide instructions, do not feed animals without permission and avoid single-use plastic. Return to the hotel for a comfortable evening in the old city.

The final touring day is a chance to revisit a favourite view or choose a small optional experience. Confirm departure arrangements early, especially if the next transfer connects with a flight. Pack purchases carefully, keep documents separate and allow time for traffic. The coordinator remains available for restaurant recommendations, airport questions and any last-minute practical support.

DAY 7

Title: Chiang Mai to Phuket

Location: Chiang Mai, Phuket

Description: Enjoy a final northern breakfast before transferring to the airport for your flight south. Phuket welcomes you with humid sea air, forested slopes and the sound of long-tail boats along the coast. After hotel check-in, keep the afternoon open for a first beach visit or a swim. Your representative will brief you about the next day's sea excursion and the expected weather. In the evening, choose between a fresh seafood restaurant, a quiet beach walk or the colourful streets of Old Phuket Town if you are staying nearby. Do not overpack the evening; this coast is at its most enjoyable when experienced slowly. Overnight in Phuket.

Allow the day to unfold around the water, weather and the interests of the group. A boat crew or local guide will explain boarding, swimming boundaries and the return time. Guests should protect phones, use sun protection and avoid touching coral or wildlife. The evening is reserved for an easy meal and a review of the next departure or transfer details.

DAY 8

Title: Phuket island escape

Location: Phuket, Andaman Sea

Description: Take an early breakfast before joining a licensed island boat for a day on the Andaman Sea. The route is subject to marine conditions, but typically includes scenic limestone islands, sheltered lagoons, swimming or snorkelling stops and time on a quiet beach. Listen to the crew's safety instructions and use the provided life jacket whenever required. Lunch is arranged during the excursion according to the confirmed programme. Return to Phuket in the afternoon and allow time to shower and rest. Those who prefer to stay on land can take a coastal drive and visit a viewpoint instead. Finish with a sunset dinner and one last view of the warm sea.

This nature-focused day benefits from patience. Wildlife and landscapes cannot be scheduled like a show, so the guide will prioritise respectful observation over rushed sightings. Wear muted, comfortable clothing, carry water and follow the sanctuary or park rules. A quiet lunch and a slower return make the experience enjoyable for both children and adults.

DAY 9

Title: Phuket farewell and departure

Location: Phuket

Description: Have breakfast and enjoy a quiet final morning. Depending on your flight, you may browse Old Phuket Town, buy local snacks or take a final walk by the beach. Check out according to the hotel's policy and keep passports, medicines and valuables in hand luggage. Your representative will transfer you to Phuket International Airport with time for international check-in. As you leave Thailand, carry home the contrast between Bangkok's river temples, Chiang Mai's mountain traditions and Phuket's open horizon. The journey ends with our thanks and an invitation to return for the quieter islands, northern villages or culinary regions that were not part of this first circuit.

The journey continues with enough time for photographs, local food and conversations about the region. Road conditions may affect the exact order of stops, and the coordinator will keep the group informed. Guests should keep a light jacket available, especially in mountain areas, and avoid overpacking the day bag. The evening provides time to rest before the next transfer.
