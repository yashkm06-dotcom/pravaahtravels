DAY-WISE ITINERARY

DAY 1

Title: Welcome to Bangkok

Location: Bangkok

Description: Arrive in Bangkok and receive a private welcome at Suvarnabhumi Airport. Your driver will transfer you to the hotel while your coordinator confirms the details of the honeymoon plan. At sunset, choose a quiet first dinner with Thai flavours and time to talk about the days ahead. The goal is not to begin with a packed tour, but to let the journey start gently. Overnight in Bangkok and rest well after the flight.

The first day is deliberately light so guests can recover from their journey and settle into the local rhythm. Keep passports and medicines in hand luggage, drink water after the flight and use the hotel team for practical questions. A short neighbourhood walk is enough if arrival is late. The coordinator will share the next morning's departure time, breakfast arrangement and any clothing guidance for temples or nature activities.

DAY 2

Title: Bangkok temples and riverside evening

Location: Bangkok

Description: Explore Bangkok's historic landmarks with a private or small-group guide. Visit the Grand Palace area, Wat Pho and Wat Arun, allowing time for photographs and the details of each site. The water, temple silhouettes and city lights create a memorable first full day together. If you prefer a quieter evening, your coordinator can arrange a restaurant away from the busiest tourist streets. Overnight in Bangkok.

Guests should carry only what they need for the day and keep valuables secure. Evening plans remain flexible, allowing the group to choose a market, a simple dinner or an early night after the sightseeing.

DAY 3

Title: Bangkok at your own pace and fly to Phuket

Guests who prefer a quieter experience can spend more time at the main site while others continue with the guide. Local food, shade breaks and responsible photography are part of the experience.
